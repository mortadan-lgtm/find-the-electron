How to read this page
Station · A = why the beam bends (electric field), B = the circle (magnetic field), C = balancing the fields.
Level · from the 3 starting questions: 1 = needs support, 2 = secure, 3 = stretch. It sets how much processing they do.
Role · trial = runs the readings, processor = fills the coloured columns, leader = leads the team and processes.
Now · where they are: station tab (predict, explore, experiment, processing, explain), progress check, final tube, Millikan, reflect, done.
Min · minutes since they started. Amber row and red number = behind the 30-minute plan.
Start Q · the 3 starting questions in order (Q1 electric field, Q2 magnetic field, Q3 balance): ✓ right, ✗ wrong.
SC start · SC check · SC end · their self-rating on the 3 success criteria at the start, at the progress check and at the end. not yet getting there confident not rated yet.
Data mark · questions right first time in "Make sense of it" (their own processing).
Right 1st try · all questions answered right first time, out of those attempted.
q/m · m / kg · their final values from the Thomson tube and Millikan. Exit · the exit question ✓ or ✗.
Station cards · who is in each room, with role and level, and how many readings are recorded and processed. × removes a student from that room.
Top boxes · class size, finished, behind plan, class mean q/m, and whether the final is locked 🔒 or open 🔓.

30-minute plan used for "behind": starting point by 4 min, predict by 6, explore by 9, experiment by 13, processing by 18, explain by 21, final by 28, reflection by 30.
